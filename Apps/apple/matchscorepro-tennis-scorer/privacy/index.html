Privacy Policy

MatchScorePro Tennis Scorer for Apple Watch

Effective date: May 24, 2026

MatchScorePro Tennis Scorer is a watch-only scorekeeping app for tennis, pickleball, and ping-pong. The app does not require an account, does not use advertising, does not use third-party analytics, and does not sell personal data.

Information We Collect

MatchScorePro Tennis Scorer does not collect personal information from you and does not transmit your match data to the developer or to third-party servers.

Local Match and Settings Data

The app may store information locally on your Apple Watch so that the scorer can function properly. This local information may include:

  • Player or team names entered by you
  • Selected sport and match preset
  • Current match score and match state
  • Undo and redo history for the current match
  • Serving indicator state
  • App settings such as colors, haptics, voice announcements, and display options

This information stays on your Apple Watch unless you remove it by resetting the match, changing settings, deleting the app, or otherwise clearing app data through the device.

Voice Announcements and Haptics

Optional voice announcements and haptic feedback are generated on the device using Apple Watch system capabilities. The app does not record your voice, access your microphone, or send voice-related data to the developer.

Accounts, Cloud Sync, and Companion Apps

The current version of MatchScorePro Tennis Scorer does not require an account, does not provide cloud sync, and does not include a phone companion app. Match state and settings are stored locally on the watch.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party advertising networks, third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or profiling services.

Apple may provide App Store, TestFlight, crash, or diagnostic information to developers according to Apple's own privacy policies and user settings.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal data collected by this app because the app does not collect such data.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local match and settings data remains on your Apple Watch until you reset match data, change settings, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Because this data is stored locally on your device, the developer does not have access to it and cannot delete it on your behalf.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
