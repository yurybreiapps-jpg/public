Privacy Policy

Racquet Sports Match Scorekeeper for iPhone and iPad

Effective date: May 28, 2026

Racquet Sports Match Scorekeeper is a native iPhone and iPad scorekeeping app for tennis, pickleball, and ping-pong. The app does not require an account, does not use advertising, does not use third-party analytics, and does not sell personal data.

Information We Collect

Racquet Sports Match Scorekeeper does not collect personal information from you and does not transmit your match data to the developer or to third-party servers.

Local Match, Player, and Settings Data

The app may store information locally on your iPhone or iPad so that the scorer, history, player profiles, and settings can function properly. This local information may include:

  • Player or team names entered by you
  • Player profile details such as preferred sport, handedness, club, and notes
  • Selected sport, match type, and match preset
  • Current match score, match state, timer, and serving indicator state
  • Undo and redo history for the active match
  • Completed match history, scores, winners, and optional notes
  • App settings such as voice announcements, display preferences, and defaults

This information stays on your device unless you remove it by deleting matches, clearing app data, uninstalling the app, or otherwise using device-level data controls.

Voice Announcements

Optional voice announcements are generated on the device using Apple system text-to-speech capabilities. The app does not record your voice, access your microphone, or send voice-related data to the developer.

Network Access and External Links

Core scoring, match history, statistics, player profiles, and settings work offline. If you choose to open support email, a website link, a privacy policy link, a native share sheet, or an Apple rating prompt, those actions may use Apple system apps or services according to your device settings and Apple's privacy policies.

Accounts, Cloud Sync, Advertising, and Analytics

The current version does not require user accounts, does not provide developer cloud sync, does not include advertising SDKs, and does not include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Apple may provide App Store, TestFlight, crash, or diagnostic information to developers according to Apple's own privacy policies and your Apple device settings.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal data collected by this app because the app does not collect such data from your device.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local match, player, and settings data remains on your iPhone or iPad until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Because this data is stored locally on your device, the developer does not have access to it and cannot delete it on your behalf.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
