Privacy Policy

ZEN Tile Flow for Apple Watch

Effective date: May 26, 2026

ZEN Tile Flow is a watch-only pipe puzzle game for Apple Watch. The app does not require an account, does not use advertising, does not use third-party analytics, does not track users, and does not sell personal data.

Information We Collect

ZEN Tile Flow does not collect personal information from you and does not transmit your gameplay data, progress, or settings to the developer or to third-party servers.

Local Game Progress and Settings

The app may store information locally on your Apple Watch so the game can function properly and preserve your progress. This local information may include:

  • Current level and puzzle state
  • Tile positions, rotations, and mirror states
  • Total solved flows and completed levels
  • Current run or random puzzle seed
  • Music, sound, motion, contrast, and flow-assist settings

This information is stored locally on your Apple Watch using Apple system storage and is not sent to the developer in the current version of the app.

Music, Sound, and Haptics

ZEN Tile Flow may play local background music and sound effects and may use Apple Watch haptics for gameplay feedback. These features operate on your device. The app does not record audio, access your microphone, or send audio or haptic data to the developer.

Accounts, Cloud Sync, and Companion Apps

The current version of ZEN Tile Flow does not require an account, does not provide cloud sync, and does not include a phone companion app. Game progress and settings are stored locally on the watch.

Purchases

The current Apple Watch version of ZEN Tile Flow does not include in-app purchases or subscriptions. If purchases are added in a future version, payment processing will be handled by Apple through the App Store, and this policy will be updated as needed.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party advertising networks, third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or profiling services.

Apple may provide App Store, TestFlight, crash, or diagnostic information to developers according to Apple's own privacy policies and your device settings.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal data collected by this app because the app does not collect such data.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local game progress and settings remain on your Apple Watch until you reset progress in the app, change settings, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Because this data is stored locally on your device, the developer does not have access to it and cannot delete it on your behalf.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
